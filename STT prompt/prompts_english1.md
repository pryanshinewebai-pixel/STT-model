# English STT Dataset: Prompts (v1)

## Recording instructions

- One recording per block, named `<speaker-id>-<block>`.
- Pause about two seconds between lines.
- Speak naturally, in your own accent. Accent variety is useful for the model.
- If possible, record in different places (quiet room, some background noise, phone in hand, phone on a table) and mention it in the file name.
- If you make a mistake, just say the line again. No need to stop the recording.

## Block 2: Similar sounding words (`<id>-P`)

Read each pair slowly, one word at a time, then read the sentence.

1. Ship, sheep. The sheep is near the ship.
2. Live, leave. I leave the house and live nearby.
3. Bat, bad, bed. The bad bat is on the bed.
4. Cut, cat, cot. The cat sat on the cot.
5. Pen, pin, pan. Put the pin and the pen in the pan.
6. Thin, sin, tin. The thin man has a tin box.
7. Very, berry, wary. The very sweet berry made him wary.
8. Right, light, write. Turn on the light and write it right.
9. Full, fool, pull. Don't be a fool, pull the door.
10. Cheap, jeep, sheep. The cheap jeep hit a sheep.

## Block 14: Free speech (`<id>-F`)

No script. Speak for about one minute on each topic in a natural way. Pauses, "um" and restarts are all fine.

1. Describe your morning routine.
2. Talk about your favourite food and how it is made.
3. Tell a short story about a trip you remember.
4. Describe your house or your neighbourhood.
5. What do you like to do on weekends?
6. Talk about a movie, song or book you like.
7. Explain how you get to work or school.
8. What would you change about your city if you could?
